Growing Agriculture, Building a Sustainable Future

Agro Resources and Formulation Co. Limited is a trusted agricultural company committed to providing high-quality agro solutions that support farmers, improve crop yields and promote food security.

We specialize in the sourcing, processing and formulation of agricultural inputs, including fertilizers, soil enhancers and crop protection products.

Our goal is to provide farmers with the right resources and knowledge to achieve better results today and for generations to come.
